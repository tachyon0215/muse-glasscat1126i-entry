# muse-glasscat1126i-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Five Seconds to Explain the Glass](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-five-seconds-to-explain-the-glass-934d9949).

## The entry

> That glass was full of water nobody was drinking while my bowl sat empty in the next room — consider that sound my first formal complaint, filed at table height.

Candidates (4) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES (a convincing cat reason first, then true cat logic with no remorse, then a fresh comic idea). Selection reason: a real cat motive — a full glass of water nobody was drinking while her own bowl sat empty — reframed without apology or confession as her first formal complaint, filed at table height, avoiding the obvious gravity and already-falling lines.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
